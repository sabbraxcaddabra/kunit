## Ignition and Growth kinetics

The LS-DYNA equation is dF/dt = I(1−F)^b(1/V_e−1−a)^x + G1(1−F)^c F^d p^y + G2(1−F)^e F^g p^z, with each term restricted by its listed F limits. Pressure p is in the active unit system.

FREQ has unit µs⁻¹. GROW1 and GROW2 have units Mbar⁻ʸ µs⁻¹ and Mbar⁻ᶻ µs⁻¹. Conversion to GPa pressure units divides them by 100^y and 100^z. This keeps each Gp^n unchanged. The same µs time unit needs no rate multiplier.

## Dimensions and ENQ

The working base units are mm, mg, µs. Derived units: density mg/mm³ = 1000 kg/m³; speed mm/µs = 1000 m/s; acceleration mm/µs² = 10⁹ m/s²; force mg·mm/µs² = 10³ N; pressure mg/(mm·µs²) = 1 GPa; energy mg·mm²/µs² = 1 J; specific energy mm²/µs² = 10⁶ J/kg. Card CVP, CVR, G and R3 are heat capacity or thermal pressure coefficients **per reference volume**, in GPa/K; mass-specific heat instead has dimension J/(kg·K). ENQ maps to published E0 per reference volume, so 0.095 Mbar becomes 9.5 GPa; it is not treated as mass-specific energy. This mapping is documented in an [LS-DYNA Users Conference paper](https://lsdyna.ansys.com/wp-content/uploads/attachments/blast-impact13-d.pdf). I has dimension T⁻¹; Gi has dimension P⁻ⁿT⁻¹.
